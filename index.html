Welcome to Jian Tang Lab. Our research focuses on quantum materials and electronic transport in low-dimensional systems. We investigate emergent quantum phenomena in 2D materials and vdW heterostructures, where topology, electron correlations, symmetry, and lattice degrees of freedom intertwine. By combining low-temperature transport, electrostatic control, and device engineering, we aim to uncover new quantum states and advance fundamental understanding of quantum matter, while exploring new concepts for future quantum and electronic devices.
